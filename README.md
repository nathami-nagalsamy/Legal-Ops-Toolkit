# Legal Ops Toolkit

Practical templates and workflows from contract lifecycle management (CLM)
and legal operations experience — built from real process improvements
across ~2,000 annual agreements, an Ironclad/DocuSign rollout, and a
Jira-based legal intake system.

## Contents
| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `ironclad-workflow-diagram.md` | Contract approval workflow (intake → execution) |
| `jira-intake-tracker-template.md` | Legal request intake tracker structure |
| `clm-onboarding-checklist.md` | Onboarding playbook for new CLM users |

## Background
These are generalized versions of processes I designed and ran while
leading legal operations for a corporate client — including configuring
Ironclad workflows that cut contract cycle time by 65%, and building a
Jira intake tracker to monitor legal request volume and turnaround.

All data shown here is illustrative — no confidential or client information.
